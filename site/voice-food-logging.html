Hands covered in protein powder? Just finished a set? Driving home from the gym? Tap the mic, describe your meal, and AI generates the full macro breakdown. No typing. No searching. No excuses.

How Voice Logging Works

1

Tap the Mic

On the Log Food or Log Workout screen, switch to Voice mode and tap the microphone button.

2

Describe Your Meal Naturally

Speak naturally: "This is the ground turkey with rice and mixed vegetables" or "Two scoops of whey protein with almond milk." No special format needed.

3

Review Transcription

Your speech is transcribed in real-time. Review the text and tap to re-record if needed. When it looks right, hit "Generate with AI."

4

AI Generates Macros

AI parses your description, identifies foods and quantities, cross-references nutrition databases, and generates a full breakdown — calories, protein, carbs, and fat.

Voice Works for Food and Workouts

Voice logging isn't just for meals. On the Log Workout screen, tap the mic and say "30 minute run, about 200 calories" — and your workout is logged with smart macro adjustments.

🎙️ Voice + Photo Combo

Take a photo of your meal and describe it with voice for maximum accuracy. AI combines both signals.

🔄 Tap to Re-Record

Misspoke? Just tap the mic again to re-record. No need to delete and start over.

🧠 Natural Language

Say it however you want — "chicken and rice" or "about 6 ounces of grilled chicken breast with a cup of white rice and steamed broccoli."

✨ Generate with AI

One tap after recording and AI does the heavy lifting — parsing ingredients, estimating portions, and pulling nutrition data.
