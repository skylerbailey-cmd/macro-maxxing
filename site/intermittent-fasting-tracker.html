Intermittent fasting restricts when you eat. Macro tracking controls what you eat. Together, they're the most effective combination for body composition — but most apps make you use two separate tools.

Common Fasting Protocols We Support

16:8

Fast 16 hours, eat in an 8-hour window. The most popular IF protocol.

18:6

Fast 18 hours, eat in a 6-hour window. A step up for experienced fasters.

20:4

Fast 20 hours, eat in a 4-hour window. Also known as the Warrior Diet.

OMAD

One Meal A Day. A 23:1 fast with all calories in a single meal.

Custom

Set any fasting and eating window that fits your schedule.
