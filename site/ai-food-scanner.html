How It Works: 3-Tier AI Recognition

Macro Maxxing uses a sophisticated 3-tier AI system that balances speed, accuracy, and cost. Most foods are recognized instantly on your device — complex meals fall through to more powerful cloud AI.

TIER 1 — FASTEST

On-Device Vision AI + USDA Lookup

Uses Apple's Vision framework to classify food directly on your device. Matches results against the USDA FoodData Central database. Zero latency, no API costs, works offline.

TIER 2 — BALANCED

Claude Haiku + Multi-Database Cross-Reference

For complex or mixed plates, the photo is sent to Claude Haiku — a fast, cost-effective AI model. Results are cross-referenced with FatSecret and OpenFoodFacts for maximum accuracy.

TIER 3 — MOST ACCURATE

Claude Sonnet Deep Analysis

For the most challenging foods — homemade recipes, international cuisines, or highly mixed plates — Claude Sonnet provides detailed analysis with portion estimation and cooking method consideration.
