Voice Workout Logging

Just finished a set? Tap the mic and say "30 minute run, about 200 calories." AI parses your description and fills in the workout details — type, duration, and estimated calorie burn.

No fiddling with dropdowns or number pickers when you're catching your breath between sets.

How Macro Adjustments Work

1

Log your workout — choose a type (or let voice AI detect it) and enter duration + estimated calories burned.

2

Macro Maxxing calculates additional macro needs based on ISSN & ACSM sports nutrition guidelines for your workout type.

3

Your daily macro targets update in real-time. The adjustment applies to today only and resets tomorrow.

4

Toggle macro adjustments on or off per workout — you're always in control.

Saved Workout Templates

Do the same workout every Monday? Save it as a template. Next time, just tap your saved "run — 30m · 200 cal" chip and it's logged instantly.

Templates save the workout name, type, duration, and calorie estimate — everything you need for a one-tap log.
