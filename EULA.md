- Provide and improve the App's core features (fasting tracking, macro logging, food recognition)

- Transmit food photos to AI services for nutritional analysis
- Display personalized nutrition insights and meal suggestions
- Serve advertisements through Google AdMob
- Comply with legal obligations

### 5.3 Third-Party Services

The App integrates with the following third-party services, each governed by their own privacy policies and terms:

- **Firebase** (Google): Authentication, cloud data storage, file storage, and remote configuration

- **Open Food Facts**: Packaged food nutritional data
- **Google AdMob**: Advertisement delivery
